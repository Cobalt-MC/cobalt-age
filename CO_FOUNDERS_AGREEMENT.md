## 1. Purpose and equality

This Agreement governs development of CobaltMC by Founder A and Founder B as equal founding collaborators. The parties wish to share project administration while preserving their respective intellectual property rights. These terms apply equally and symmetrically to both Founders.

No party represents that mere ownership of a Modrinth page, GitHub account or organization conveys authorship of, or copyright in, the other party's code or assets.

## 2. Ownership of contributions

2.1 Each Founder retains ownership of independently authored, copyrightable source code, assets, documentation and other original material they contribute ("Individual Contributions"), except where a separate written agreement validly assigns ownership.

2.2 Jointly authored contributions remain subject to each applicable author's rights and any specific written allocation agreed by both Founders. No separate contributions register is required. If ownership of particular material is disputed, neither party shall unilaterally relicense it as exclusively owned, pending a written resolution agreed by both Founders.

2.3 Git history, source headers, design records and dated archives are evidence, but commit counts or `git blame` alone do not conclusively determine copyright ownership.

2.4 Neither Founder transfers ownership of their Individual Contributions to the other Founder or to the CobaltMC organization by signing this Agreement.

## 3. Conditional license for joint development

3.1 During an active collaboration, each Founder grants the other a **non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, purpose-limited contractual license** to reproduce, adapt, build and incorporate their eligible proprietary Individual Contributions solely into jointly approved CobaltMC development and Official Releases.

3.2 This license permits distribution of jointly approved Official Releases to end users under CobaltMC's duly adopted public proprietary license. It does not permit distributing source or binaries under a different license, publishing unauthorized forks, or using the other Founder's proprietary material in any other project, organization, product or Modrinth page.

3.3 The license in clause 3.1 is **prospective and terminable** under clauses 6 and 7. Permissions validly granted under existing GPL, AGPL, other third-party licenses, or separately executed irrevocable grants remain unaffected.

3.4 No Founder may grant another person or company broader rights to the other Founder's Individual Contributions without that Founder's prior written consent.

## 4. Joint administration: no unilateral removal

4.1 Both Founders shall be recognized as founders and active developers on the relevant project pages while they remain participants. Each shall hold the highest mutually available administrative role, subject to the platform's technical limits and the safeguards agreed below.

4.2 Without the other Founder's prior **written consent**, neither Founder may: (a) remove or suspend the other's project membership or admin rights; (b) transfer project or organization ownership; (c) delete or replace the official page/repository; (d) change the project license; (e) alter the other's historical authorship; or (f) confer unilateral control of those actions upon a third party.

4.3 If platform functionality permits one person to perform such an action without a second approval, that technical capability **does not constitute contractual authorization**. The Founders shall, where practicable, configure the platform to minimize unilateral changes and maintain secure independent copies of project records.

4.4 Emergency measures to comply with law, prevent a demonstrable security compromise or respond to abuse may be taken only to the minimum extent necessary; the other Founder must be notified promptly and any continuing restriction must be independently reviewed or mutually agreed. This exception may not be used to disguise a personal dispute as a security incident.

## 5. Decisions that require both Founders

The Founders must approve in writing: (i) a release changing the license; (ii) the admission of any co-owner or administrator with equivalent control; (iii) transfer of project/repository assets; (iv) commercial licensing or sale of shared assets; (v) changes to ownership allocations; (vi) use of one Founder's proprietary contributions outside CobaltMC; and (vii) termination or amendment of this Agreement, except where termination is expressly permitted here.

Routine maintenance and release work may be delegated by a jointly approved written workflow. Neither Founder may use a deadlock to claim unilateral ownership.

## 6. Unauthorized exclusion and breach

6.1 A unilateral removal or demotion prohibited by clause 4 is a **material breach**. The excluded Founder may serve written notice describing the breach and requesting restoration of rights.

6.2 Upon receipt of that notice, the parties shall **pause new Official Releases containing the complaining Founder's proprietary Individual Contributions**, except a release expressly approved in writing by that Founder or a legally mandatory security fix. This interim pause is a contractual safeguard, not a retroactive cancellation of rights granted to the public.

6.3 The alleged breaching Founder has **10 calendar days after receipt of a valid notice** to cure the breach by restoring access, correcting notices and confirming the agreed governance in writing, unless the parties agree to an extension.

6.4 If a material breach is not cured in that period, the excluded Founder may terminate the limited prospective license granted under clause 3 for their Individual Contributions by written notice specifying an effective date not earlier than the end of the cure period.

6.5 From the effective termination date, the breaching Founder and any project or organization operating under that Founder's authority must not issue, upload, update, rebrand or otherwise newly distribute a release containing the excluded Founder's proprietary Individual Contributions, except under a separate written authorization or a prior valid independent license. Code and assets must be removed or independently replaced before such releases are made.

6.6 These contractual restrictions bind **the signatories**, and any additional entity that separately accepts them. They do **not** automatically bind non-signing recipients who possess independent legal permissions, nor revoke previously valid GPL/AGPL grants or end-user use rights for lawfully acquired releases.

6.7 Historical credit must be preserved even when all licensed code is replaced in future releases. A Founder may cease to be listed as an active maintainer but must not be falsely erased from the historical record.

## 7. Voluntary departure: each Founder may withdraw future-use authorization

7.1 Either Founder may voluntarily leave CobaltMC by giving **30 calendar days' written notice**. The departing Founder may elect in the notice either:

- **Withdrawal:** terminate the clause 3 license for the departing Founder's proprietary Individual Contributions for all new releases from the effective departure date; or
- **Continuation:** grant a specific, signed extension identifying which contributions, projects, permitted uses and duration remain authorized.

7.2 If the departing Founder elects Withdrawal, the remaining Founder shall not include the departing Founder's proprietary Individual Contributions in **new releases published after the effective date**, whether on the original page or on any other project or page administered by the remaining Founder, unless a separate valid permission applies.

7.3 The remaining Founder may maintain or republish no new builds containing that material after the effective date without permission. Existing lawfully distributed end-user copies and any rights already irreversibly granted to other recipients are unaffected. Whether an already published download remains hosted must be separately agreed, and this Agreement does not purport to revoke an independent public license.

7.4 If the departing Founder does not expressly choose an option, **Withdrawal is the default for future new releases**, subject to the exceptions in clause 8.

7.5 The same right of election belongs to each Founder, irrespective of who wrote more code or initiated the departure.

## 8. Previously licensed and third-party content

8.1 GPLv3, AGPLv3, LGPL, MIT, Apache and other existing licenses continue to govern any material legitimately received under those licenses. Neither Founder can impose new proprietary restrictions on a code portion already validly licensed to recipients under terms that prohibit such restrictions.

8.2 Historical Cobalt Age releases previously made available under GPLv3 retain their GPLv3 permissions. A CobaltMC release can be proprietary only if the relevant rights holders have authorized that choice and all incorporated code/assets permit it.

8.3 The contractual Withdrawal mechanism does not override rights in GPL/AGPL code already held by a Founder or others. A party wishing to prevent any future reuse of particular code should not first license that code irrevocably to the public under AGPL/GPL.

8.4 Third-party content must be listed in a license inventory and retain its required notices. No participant may claim exclusive ownership of another author's work.

## 9. Public source and official binary distribution

9.1 The Founders may make the proprietary source publicly readable on GitHub while restricting copying, modification, redistribution and incorporation beyond rights expressly permitted or required by law and GitHub's Terms of Service.

9.2 Before publication, both Founders shall adopt `LICENSE` and verify that each code/asset owner has validly consented. Publication of new material under this proprietary license does not itself make the work "open source" under the Open Source Definition.

9.3 No Contributor will be required to publish a private development history, unrelated branches, unpublished research or other proprietary work outside the Covered Material authorized for publication.

## 10. Preservation, accounting and verification

10.1 The Founders shall maintain an accessible project archive of release artifacts, attribution notices and ownership records. Both shall be able to preserve evidence of their contributions and agreed releases.

10.2 No separate contributions register is required. For purposes of resolving any ownership dispute, the Founders may rely on the repository history, original source files, copyright notices, contemporaneous communications and other available evidence. Each Founder shall keep an independent signed copy of this Agreement and approved changes. Third-party license notices and other legally required records must still be preserved.

10.3 Upon departure or breach, the parties shall document which proprietary contributions are withdrawn and the replacement status of any code/asset planned for use in future releases. Neither shall falsely claim that a superficial reformat or rename makes the other person's protected expression independently authored.

## 11. Disputes, remedies and limits

11.1 Written notice shall be sent to the designated email address(es) above and is effective upon demonstrable receipt. A Discord message may be used as additional evidence, but is not the exclusive notice method. If a designated address is invalid, the parties shall use another demonstrably received written method.

11.2 The parties shall first attempt good-faith resolution within 15 calendar days of notice. If unresolved, the dispute shall be handled under the chosen governing law and forum, without removing rights to seek urgent judicial protection where legally available.

11.3 Actual damages and injunctive or specific relief may be sought **only where available under applicable law**. Nothing in this Agreement makes a disputed breach automatically a crime or guarantees a takedown by Modrinth or GitHub.

11.4 If any clause is unenforceable, remaining provisions survive so far as permitted by law. An invalid restriction must not be construed to revoke a license already validly granted to a third party.

11.5 Changes must be in writing and signed by both Founders. This Agreement is the entire agreement on its subject matter, except separately identified signed instruments and existing licenses that retain legal effect.
